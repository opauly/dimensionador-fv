How it works

Three steps. Zero new hardware.

The same pipeline that has been sending Pauly & Co.'s own customers a report every Monday for months — now available whether you're watching one home or a whole fleet of installs.

01

Upload

Export a CSV straight from VRM, or connect the VRM API for automatic pulls. No changes to Node-RED, no re-flashing a Cerbo GX — this reads data your equipment is already generating.

02

Process

We parse daily energy, battery cycling, alarms, and outages; score system health 0–100; and let Claude write the week in plain language — not just a wall of numbers.

03

Deliver

A branded PDF lands in your inbox automatically — or your customer's, if you're managing more than one site. Pick a range past a month and it becomes a monthly Overview instead — same report, right granularity.

What's inside

Every section the report
actually contains.

Twelve modules, computed once and shown consistently everywhere. Two of them read differently depending on the range you pick — try the toggle.

Past 31 days, the report switches itself — no setting to remember.
Scoring

Health score

0–100, alongside solar generation, grid independence, and events for the period — one number a homeowner can actually track over time.

Narrative

AI narrative

A short paragraph explaining what happened this period — plain language, not a wall of numbers.A paragraph that describes how the system trended across segments — improving, worsening, or steady — not one lump total.

Adapts to range

Daily solar vs. consumptionSolar vs. consumption

Compares daily solar production against household consumption for each day in this period.Compares solar production against household consumption for each segment of this period.

Energy mix

Where your energy came from

Shows how much of your energy came from solar panels, batteries, and the utility grid — one donut, always up to date.

Battery

Battery health

Tracks how well your batteries charged and discharged throughout this period — cycling stress, float days, voltage range.

Grid

Grid quality

Measures the quality and stability of the utility grid supply at your site — frequency and voltage extremes, not just outages.

Events

Outages & alarms

Logs grid outages and alarm episodes recorded by the system during this period — the same definition your live monitoring uses.

Adapts to range

Battery SOC timeline

Shows the daily high and low battery charge level — a dip below 20% signals heavy use.Shows each segment's high and low battery charge level — a dip below 20% signals heavy use.

Performance

Solar performance

Compares real solar production to the theoretical maximum, based on your panel capacity and the sunlight actually available.

Weather

Weather context

Local conditions for this period — cloud cover and rain directly reduce solar output, so the report accounts for them.

Always weekly

4-week solar trend

Compares production across the past 4 weeks to spot seasonal patterns — fixed at this cadence on purpose, whatever range you picked.

Savings

Estimated savings

Estimated electricity cost avoided this period by using solar instead of buying from the grid — a real number, never a placeholder.
